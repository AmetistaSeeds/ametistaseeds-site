3. No cookies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Fonts, images and videos are loaded directly from this website, not from third-party servers.

4. Anonymous visit statistics

To know which pages are used and whether the website works properly, I count visits anonymously. Only daily totals are stored: the page viewed, the country (derived by the server, without storing the IP address), the website the visit came from (domain only), technical error messages, and clicks on videos and on links to music services. No IP addresses, identifiers or profiles are stored. I also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking people. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running and improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Links to other websites

This website contains plain links, for example to babinoli.com, YouTube and music services (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Amazon Music). Only when you click such a link are you taken to that provider's site, whose privacy policy then applies. No data is sent to these providers before that.

6. Contact form and email

If you write to me through the contact form or by email, I receive your name (if given), your email address and your message. Form messages and emails to addresses at ametistaseeds.com are forwarded by Cloudflare to my mailbox at Google (Gmail), where Google's privacy policy also applies. I use this data only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete it once it is no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep it.

WhatsApp. If you click the WhatsApp button, you leave this site and WhatsApp opens (WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland, part of the Meta group). This site sends nothing to WhatsApp before that click. When you write to me there, WhatsApp processes your data under its own policy (whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy-eea), which may include transfers to the USA. I use your number and messages only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). If you prefer, use the form or email instead.
